1. Executive Summary & Novelty

Predicting corporate distress in emerging economies like India poses unique challenges due to diverse corporate governance, cyclical commodity sectors, high leverage in capital-intensive industries, and the legal framework governed by the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) 2016 and Reserve Bank of India (RBI) prudential norms.

How This System Differs from Existing Academic Systems:

Dimension Traditional Existing Systems Our System
Classification Granularity Binary only (0: Bankrupt, 1: Non-Bankrupt) 4-Tier Granular Classification: Bankrupt, Probably Bankrupt, Financial Distress, Healthy
Data Scope US / European datasets (e.g. Altman Z-Score Polish data) Audited Indian filings across 999 Indian firms over a 20-year horizon (2006–2026)
Data Leakage Prevention Random row-wise train/test splits (temporal & company leakage) Strict Company-Level Split: 798 train firms, 201 completely unseen test firms (0% leakage)
Explainability Black-box predictions with no regulator audit trail Game-Theoretic TreeSHAP (XAI) calculating exact marginal attributions for every metric
Operational Testing Static historical testing only Real-Time Live Market Ingestion via Yahoo Finance: evaluates live balance sheets of any NSE/BSE stock
User Experience Complex command scripts or fragmented multi-page UI Unified Single-Screen Streamlit Dashboard displaying verdict, ratios, and SHAP on one page

2. Dataset Overview & Feature Schema

  • Dataset: Indian Company Financial Dataset.csv
  • Observations: 8,991 Company-Year records across 999 Indian firms.
  • Audit Time Horizon: 2006 to 2026 (captures global financial crisis, demonetization, IBC enactment, COVID-19, and recovery).
  • Target Classes (4 Tiers):
  • 0: Bankrupt (Net worth fully eroded, defaulted on loans, liquidation/NCLT proceedings)
  • 1: Financial Distress (Early-stage operational deterioration, declining interest coverage, margin compression)
  • 2: Probably Bankrupt (Multi-quarter default, recurring operating losses, high leverage exceeding 4.0x)
  • 3: Healthy (Comfortable interest coverage > 2.5x, sustainable leverage, consistent positive operating cash flow)

63 Engineered Financial Indicators:

  1. Core Accounting Fundamentals: Sales, Raw Material Cost, Employee Cost, Other Expenses, Depreciation, Interest, Profit Before Tax (PBT), Tax, Net Profit, Equity Capital, Reserves, Borrowings, Total Liabilities, Total Assets, Receivables, Inventory, Cash & Bank.
  2. Cash Flow Fundamentals: Cash from Operating Activities, Cash from Investing Activities, Cash from Financing Activities, Net Cash Flow.
  3. Core Solvency & Profitability Ratios:
  4. Interest Coverage Ratio = Operating Profit (EBIT) / Interest Expense
  5. Debt-to-Equity Ratio = Total Borrowings / (Equity Capital + Reserves)
  6. Operating Margin = Operating Profit / Total Sales
  7. Return on Assets (ROA) = Net Profit / Total Assets
  8. Return on Equity (ROE) = Net Profit / Net Worth
  9. Working Capital Ratio = Working Capital / Total Assets
  10. Cash-to-Debt Ratio = Cash & Bank / Total Borrowings
  11. Dynamic Multi-Year Growth Metrics: 1-Year & 3-Year CAGR for Sales, Net Profit, Total Debt, Total Assets, Cash Flow, and historical volatility.

4. Explainable AI (TreeSHAP) Architecture

In compliance with banking audits (RBI stress testing) and insolvency court proceedings (NCLT under IBC 2016), black-box predictions are unacceptable. We integrated TreeSHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) based on cooperative game theory:

TreeSHAP Game-Theoretic Shapley Equation:
φi(x) = ∑S ⊆ F \ {i} [ |S|! (|F| - |S| - 1)! / |F|! ] × [ fx(S ∪ {i}) - fx(S) ]

Top Mathematical Solvency Drivers Across 8,991 Filings:

  1. Interest Coverage Ratio (Mean |SHAP| = 1.5152): The #1 determinant. Companies unable to cover annual interest from EBIT are overwhelmingly propelled into distress.
  2. Operating Profit Margin (Mean |SHAP| = 0.5387): Negative operating margins indicate bleeding core cash flows.
  3. Debt-to-Equity Ratio (Mean |SHAP| = 0.4069): Financial leverage above 3.5x leaves no buffer for macroeconomic or demand downturns.
  4. Return on Assets (ROA) (Mean |SHAP| = 0.1341): Asset productivity and earnings yield.
  5. Return on Equity (ROE) (Mean |SHAP| = 0.1331): Capital erosion velocity.

5. Real-Time Live Market Ingestion Engine

Implemented in live_data_fetcher.py: * Accepts any ticker symbol (auto-normalizes Indian tickers to .NS for National Stock Exchange or .BO for Bombay Stock Exchange). * Fetches the latest published Balance Sheet, Income Statement, and Cash Flows from Yahoo Finance (yfinance). * Extracts raw statements, computes all 63 ratios and growth trajectories, handles missing items via indian_imputer.pkl, and delivers: 1. Live risk verdict and 4-tier confidence percentages. 2. Solvency KPI metrics grid. 3. Real-time TreeSHAP waterfall/bar attribution chart.
